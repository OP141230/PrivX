# About PrivX

PrivX is a private search engine that aggregates results from multiple
{{link('search engines', 'preferences')}} while storing absolutely nothing
about you — no logs, no tracking, no noise.

## Why PrivX?

- PrivX doesn't generate a profile about you or what you search for.
- PrivX never shares your queries with third parties.
- PrivX can't be used to compromise you — your searches stay yours.
- Zero logs. Zero tracking. Zero noise.

## How do I set it as my default search engine?

PrivX supports [OpenSearch], so you can add it directly to your browser.

- [Firefox] — click the search bar, then "Add PrivX"
- [Microsoft Edge] — go to Settings → Privacy → Address bar and search
- [Chromium]-based browsers — navigate to PrivX, then go to browser search engine settings

## How does it work?

PrivX routes your search queries through multiple search engines simultaneously,
merges the results, and returns them to you — without ever storing your query,
your IP address, or anything else. Your search leaves no trace.
